Welcome to the About Page!

I’m Noël Eldred, the graphic designer behind Stockpot Designs. As a designer, I aim to help people refine the branding and visuals that dress up their incredible ideas, products, events andso much more. While I’ve always lovedart and the process of creating, it wasn’t until highschool that I realizedthat I could use my passion to uplift other’s ideas through graphic design!

While most of my experience is in print design, such as posters, book covers, postcards, business collateral, and similar products, I enjoy designing for just about anything andeverything. A few of my specialties are accessible design, logo design, illustration and branding. In my opinion, one of the best ways for your business to stand out is through accessible design and strong branding. The visual appearance of a brand helps buildrecognition and a positive connection with your audience while making that brand accessible creates a friendly and easy-to-use environment for everyone.

Apart from design and art, I also enjoy consuming stories of all kinds. Whether that be through TV shows, books, music, games, or things that I randomly became curious about. Although, I have to admit, I prefer the mystery genre over all the others. I do love a good puzzle!

As a designer, it’s my personal goal to find and learn as many styles as possible to continue to grow my design skillset and help to capture each client’s vision for their project. If you’re in the market for a graphic designer for a project of yours, I’d love to hear all about it! Feel free to contact me through one of the methods listed on the Contact page!

-Noël Eldred

Q & A
Question Answer
What programs do you use in the design process? My main programs are in the Adobe Suite, such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign. But I am able to learn other programs quickly if necessary!
How many times will we have to meet for my project? It truely depends on the scale of your project. Smaller projects, like a poster, sticker sheet, or book cover will only require about 3 meetings. Larger scope projects such as branding or a project with multiple products being designed will require a lot more meetings to ensure consistency and quality.
What do I need to have prepared for out first meeting? Nothing! Well, not entirely. The first meeting is the brainstroming session where we discuss the intention and purpose of the project. Prior to the meeting you should have an idea of what your looking for, the goal of the project, your audience, the time that the project needs to be completed by, and your budget. Although, I will never complain if you bring ideas, rough sketches, or inspiration!
Will I need to send my files to the printer myself, or will you? Typically, I design and send print-ready PDFs to the client for them to get printed at their preferred location. However, If you are not sure how to get your project to print, I can help you with this process.
Do you do digital designs too? Absolutely! I can help design your digital ads, social media posts, and online documents.
